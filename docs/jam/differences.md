# Differences from Jam

**The official app is Band Desktop since 0.4.14** (installed here 2026-10-08): this app says Band wherever it shows the name, and has 0.4.14's Teams section. Rows checked against 0.4.10 keep its words.

This app does what the official Jam Desktop does, and looks like ZCode (`docs/DESIGN.md`). This page lists every place where what it does or says differs from Jam, and why, so nothing Jam has is changed or dropped silently. Differences of look alone (layout, spacing, colour, type) follow ZCode and are recorded in `docs/DESIGN.md`; features not built yet are the open rows of `docs/jam/features.md`. A change that makes or finds a difference adds its row here in the same change, and a fix removes it.

Checked against Jam Desktop 0.4.10 on Windows, clicked through read-only on 2026-09-19, and against its user guide. The captures stay on the machine that made them: they show a real account. On 2026-09-20 those captures were read again, control tree and pixels both, to settle the rows that said what Jam does was not checked; the rows that remain to fix each wait on a feature this app has still to build, or on a press in Jam Desktop that would act on a real account.

**Kept** is a deliberate difference, with the reason it earns its place. **To fix** will match Jam. **Not checked** is a choice that Jam's own behaviour could not settle yet (it needs several rooms, a state not seen, or a press in Jam Desktop that would act on a real account); it is checked when it can be. A room with an agent was seen on 2026-09-19, with a question and a permission request waiting in it.
