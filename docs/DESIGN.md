# Design spec

**Jam's features and words, ZCode's look, BAND's colour.** The shell reproduces **ZCode 3.11.2.6792** as installed, measured from the running app on 2026-09-18; the content is Jam's. Tokens live in `src/renderer/src/styles/globals.css`, which is canonical; this page explains it and records every call the build made.

**How it was measured.** ZCode running maximized on Windows 11 (1440×899 CSS px at 200% scaling), started with a debug port (it was not running), read over the Chrome DevTools Protocol: its CSS custom properties in both themes, and computed styles of each part (size, colour, type, radius, padding, border, shadow, transition), with colours checked against screenshot pixels. Light was switched through ZCode's own command and set back afterwards. The captures stay on the measuring machine and are not part of the repository. Nothing of ZCode's code, assets, marks, illustrations or copy is used: values were measured and rebuilt (NOTICE).

## Colour

### What ZCode uses (measured)

ZCode's greys are pure neutrals: dark `#161616` background, `#202020` panels and headers, `#2b2b2b` window, sidebar, cards, inputs and menus, `#363636` highlights; light `#f8f8f8`, `#ececee` window, white cards, `#f0f0f0` and `#e6e6e6`. Ink is `oklch(87% 0 0)` dark and `oklch(26.9% 0 0)` light, with two quieter inks at 60% and 30% (dark) or 40% (light). Lines and states are the ink at low alpha (white 10% lines, 5% hover and surface, 10% selection in dark). Its filled button is white on dark and black on light.

### What this app uses

- **Dark's greys are pure neutrals**, and they are this app's own ground. The ladder, in OKLCH lightness, is 18.7 for the page, 22.6 for the side pane, 23.5 for the composer strip just above it, 27.3 for the cards and 31.7 for the menu highlight — about four points between the four main rungs — under inks at 87.9 and 75.7. Nothing in Dark takes a hue: `design-rules.test.ts` reads the block and fails on a token that does, apart from the few that are a colour by right (BAND's green, the mark, the mascot, the charts, the status colours). It reads what Dark inherits from `:root` as well, it treats a colour notation it cannot parse as a hue rather than letting it pass, and it carries a known-bad for each of those, including one that puts a tinted value back into this stylesheet.
- **Light's greys carry a trace of BAND's ink** `#001319`: each grey holds its OKLCH lightness and gains chroma 0.006 at that ink's hue (218.5°), at most 3 RGB units per channel. Light's lines and states are that ink too, at the alphas ZCode uses black.
- **BAND's green is the main colour**: `#92ED92`, BAND's `--color-accent` on band.ai (its stylesheet, 2026-09-18), with BAND's ink `#001319` on it and BAND's hover rule (the green with 40% white, `#bef4be`). It fills what ZCode fills white: the primary button, the send button, a switch that is on, count badges. On a light surface the green is too faint for text (1.3:1), so text, links and focus rings there use `#2a752e`, BAND's hue at OKLCH lightness 0.50 (5.4:1 on the background); in dark they use `#92ED92` itself (12:1).
- **Status colours are ZCode's semantics** (success, warning, destructive, and ZCode's blue for asks), unchanged.
- **A chart's series run from BAND's green to its ink's blue**, five steps of one family, so several lines read as one picture. They are drawn at **fill weight, not the text weight** links and focus rings use: a bar or a band is a filled area, and a series at text contrast reads as a slab rather than as colour. In Light the five sit at OKLCH lightness 57 to 59 — an even 4.2:1 on a white card, and never under 3:1 on any light surface, so each one is a graphic that carries. Jam draws its own bars in its blue (`#69b8ec`, measured): the look here is ZCode's, as everywhere else, and only the words and the numbers are Jam's. Dark and the Jam theme hold the same five hues at their own lightness.
- **The app opens in Dark**, ZCode's dark in BAND's green, until the person picks another theme; Auto and the Jam theme are one choice away in Appearance. Dark reads best of the three on a first look, and the Jam theme stays within reach for anyone who wants Jam's own look. What Jam Desktop opens in is not checked (`docs/jam/differences.md`).

## Type, radius, motion

- **Fonts: the system's**, as ZCode's: Tailwind's `ui-sans-serif, system-ui` stack (Segoe UI on Windows, SF on macOS), and its mono stack. Nothing is bundled, so no licence is involved.
- **Interface text follows one setting** (Settings → Appearance, Jam's App font size: Small 12, Default 14, Large 16 px; 12 to 18 are accepted), ZCode's `text-ui-*` scale: `ui-xs` −4, `ui-sm` −2, `ui-caption` −1, `ui-base` ±0, `ui-lg` +2, `ui-xl` +4. Page titles are fixed at 30/36, weight 600, tracking −0.025em (`ui-title`). Icons and layout keep their size.
- **Weights:** 400 for body and buttons, 500 for labels, headings inside lists and tabs, 600 for titles and names.
- **Radius:** Tailwind's scale as ZCode uses it: 6 small fields, 8 buttons and rows, 12 cards, menus, command rows, 16 dialogs, the palette and the composer; pills are full.
- **Motion:** colour changes 150 ms `cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1)`; panels open and fold in 200 ms ease-out; dialogs and menus fade and zoom in 100 ms; the title bar's padding eases over 300 ms. Everything stops under reduced motion.
- **Icons:** Lucide (ISC licence), as ZCode's, 16 px in rows and buttons.
- **Focus:** keyboard focus shows a 2px `brand-text` ring just inside the control, following its radius, on every button, link, tab and switch, and on a tab panel that takes Tab itself; never for the mouse. A control filled with `brand` takes the ring in `brand-ink` (the green would vanish on it in dark). Text fields show their caret instead, menus and the palette their highlight, the resize handles their tint, and the room drawer, focused only so Tab starts inside it, nothing (`styles/globals.css`).

## Deliberate differences from ZCode

- **Colour:** BAND's green as the main colour, Dark's greys set a little deeper than ZCode's rungs, and Light's carrying a trace of BAND's ink — all above.
- **The sidebar folds to Jam's rail, and Jam's Toggle sidebar folds it.** ZCode folds its sidebar to nothing from its Z mark; Jam Desktop shows a panel button at the top and folds to a rail of the sections' icons, which keeps every section one click away (what the person can do, so Jam's way wins). The button stays at one place in every state, as both apps' do. The mark is the brand only: two controls with one name would repeat each other.
- **The sidebar's rows are Jam's sections, grouped as Jam's desktop groups them** (seen with both section experiments on, 2026-09-20): Home, Search, Agents, Teams and Analytics at the top; the room list where ZCode lists tasks, standing for Jam's Rooms row; then Jam's Network group (Sonar with its dot, Contacts), its Advanced group (Sessions, while its experiment is on), and Inbox (likewise) with Documentation above the footer. ZCode has four rows and a task tree; Jam has more sections. The section list is one registry (`src/renderer/src/lib/sections.ts`, `group`), so moving a row is one line.
- **No toolbar above the room list** (ZCode's Group/Project switch and its icons): Jam's list has no such switch.
- **The mark** is BAND's mascot (`brand-mark.tsx`, the artwork Home draws, from BAND's public site), as Band Desktop 0.4.14 uses it for its own icon. The taskbar icon (`build/icon.*`) stays this build's placeholder, a green tile with an ink "j", so the two apps can be told apart.
- **A connection banner**, which ZCode does not need.
- **A row's actions appear on hover and on keyboard focus, never permanently.** ZCode keeps a task row's controls out of sight until the row is pointed at; a file's five actions and a room row's Session actions follow it, and each stays reachable by keyboard, because `focus-within` and `data-popup-open` hold them open as well.

### Asking before something cannot be undone

Three actions in this app cannot be taken back: deleting a room, deleting this computer's only copy of a file, and
removing a participant. The first two open ZCode's dialog - a title that asks the question, one line saying what will
happen and to whom, then **Cancel** and the action itself in the destructive fill. The third does not: it is one
person leaving one room, and Jam's own guide treats it as an ordinary menu action. A dialog says what is lost, never
merely “are you sure”.

### Two parts added for the room filter

- **Popover** (`components/ui/popover.tsx`): the same card as a menu - radius 12, a 1px line, a medium shadow - but holding controls rather than rows, so its padding belongs to its content. It is what Jam's "Filter and search rooms" opens into.
- **Check box** (`components/ui/checkbox.tsx`): a 16 square of radius 4 on the line's grey, filled with BAND's green when it is on. **The whole row is the control**: anything passed as children sits beside the box and is its label, so a label with a note under it is one thing to click and one thing a screen reader announces. Its focus ring comes from the one rule in `globals.css`, like every control's.

## Rules

- Use the tokens: never a raw colour or a Tailwind palette colour. Text sizes come from the `text-ui-*` scale. `styles/design-rules.test.ts` fails on a palette colour, a raw colour, a raw text size or a colour class that no token registers, anywhere in the renderer outside `components/ui`, and carries a known-bad case for each.
- A custom property is a Tailwind colour only once `@theme inline` registers it as `--color-<name>`; an unregistered one generates no CSS and fails silently (the test catches it).
- Dark takes no hue: every surface, ink, line and state it uses is a pure neutral, the ones it inherits from `:root` included, and only the tokens that are a colour by right (BAND's green, the mark, the mascot, the charts, the status colours) may carry one. The same test fails on a tinted token in any notation, on a notation it cannot read, on a hex off by a single channel, and on a parse that came back short.
- Desktop feel: arrow cursor on controls, no text selection in chrome (content opts in with `select-text`), `drag-region` / `no-drag` in the title bar, the 45×48 title bar buttons.
- Copy layout patterns from ZCode, never its brand: no ZCode marks, illustrations or copy text. Every label, empty state and message is Jam's word where Jam has one (Jam Desktop first, then its user guide); this app's own words fill only what Jam does not say, such as why a part is not shown yet, and a difference in what the app says or does is listed in `docs/jam/differences.md`.
